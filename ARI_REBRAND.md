# 🎨 ARI REBRAND: AI → Augmented Reality Intelligence

## 🤔 THE CONCEPT:
**AI** = Artificial Intelligence (generic, everywhere)
**ARI** = Augmented Reality Intelligence (unique, YOUR combo)

## ✅ PROS:
- **Unique** - No one else uses "ARI"
- **Descriptive** - AR + AI combined
- **Memorable** - Sounds like "Airy" (light, beauty)
- **Accurate** - Your app IS both AR and AI together

## ❌ CONS:
- **Education needed** - People know "AI", not "ARI"
- **Search issues** - People search "AI makeup", not "ARI makeup"
- **Confusion** - Might think it's a typo
