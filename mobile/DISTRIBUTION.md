# 📱 Glam Guide AI - Distribution Options

## 🆓 FREE DISTRIBUTION (No $25 Play Console Fee)

### Option 1: APK Sideloading (Easiest)
Share APK file directly with users!

**Steps:**
1. Build APK: `./mobile/build-android.sh`
2. Send file: `android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`
3. Users install by tapping APK on their phone

**Pros:**
- ✅ Completely free
- ✅ Instant distribution
- ✅ No approval process

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Users must enable "Unknown sources" in settings
- ⚠️ No automatic updates
